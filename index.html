Him & Her Bible

Help and support

Him & Her Bible is a large-print Bible app with a version made for women and a version made for men. It has highlighters, drawing, stickers, themes, study plans, verse memorizing, prayer circles, and a kind community for sisters and brothers in Christ.

Contact us

Email himandherbible@protonmail.com. We answer within 1–2 days, and we review every report of harmful content within 24 hours.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the three dots next to any post or reply, or tap any chat message. Choose Report to tell us about it, or Block to stop seeing that person. You can unblock people from your account page.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap the gear at the top right to open the menu, then tap Delete my account at the bottom. You can also find it in Edit profile. This removes your account, your posts, and any study groups you lead. Your highlights and notes on your phone are not affected.

Where are my highlights and drawings saved?

Your highlights, drawings, stickers, favorites and journal are saved on your own phone. They are not uploaded. If you delete the app, they are removed too.

Which Bible versions are included?

The King James Version (KJV) and the World English Bible (WEB). Both are in the public domain.

Someone is in danger

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911. If you or someone you know is thinking about suicide, call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, U.S.).
